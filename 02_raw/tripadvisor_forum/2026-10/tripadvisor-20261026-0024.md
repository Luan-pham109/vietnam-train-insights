Morning! We're wanting to take this train around April 4- just the regular passenger train, not the 'tourist' version. But online sites showing conflicting information - some say no trains, others that we can't book this far ahead, or we can only pay with 'cards issued from Vietnamese banks'. Any advice about easiest way to book this- we are a group of 7 so don't want to leave it until we arrive.

[c:e5b333a0f4]
Finds Baolau the best booking site, easier to understand than others, small booking fee and accepts Australian cards. The afternoon SE20 appears to be the only train selling seats at the moment, that starts in Da Nang though. The through train wanted, SE6, is selling for longer distances; the short trip wanted will not open up until closer. Just keep following the site.

[c:a8c926a0e3]
"One more vote for using baolau site to book trains."

[c:a87cf9b453]
"Yep, shorter distances open up closer to the date." Vietnam Railways prefers to sell longer distances first, which is a business strategy.
