[c:2949e93919]
If flying from Hanoi on 1/1, do not wait until the last 1-2 days to book. Once, 4 days before New Year, discovered all flights to Hue were fully booked; so were all flights to Da Nang. Almost the same on 2/1, and had to buy Business Class to Da Nang and take a car to Hue for a birthday celebration important to him/her on 3/1. Learned, and a couple of years later booked in November and made the Hue flight on a full plane. Tet fares for both trains and flights for the Tet Travel Period beginning 23/1 went on sale in the past two weeks. Seems the poster will be travelling toward HCMC by then, against the flow of people leaving HCMC for the provinces and smaller cities, and should not face problems booking a few days ahead. Fares could be higher.
