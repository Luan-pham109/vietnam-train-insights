CAVEAT: the post date is 8 April 2026, but the experience recounted is from 2007. The poster states she was a solo female traveller on the overnight trains North to South Vietnam, aged 27, back in 2007, and that it was her first time using the service and she had not done much research. Evidence summaries must carry this 2007-experience caveat.

Vietnam was safe to travel around, but she would not recommend booking the 4-bed cabins as a solo female; it did not feel safe. A man boarded along the way and took the upper-level bunk while she had the bottom. It was a weird feeling, with 4 bunks in one small space. It did not feel easy to alert someone if something happened, as no attendants were around (possibly further down the train, but trains are also very long). Going to the toilet and leaving a backpack was also not convenient (she took valuables), but she was more concerned that she was on edge the whole time and someone was peering at her from their bed, so she could not rest or sleep as she felt on guard, and it is a very long, approximately 25-hour journey. She would not do that again; she would either book the whole cabin herself or go with others. However, even if you book the whole cabin yourself, it does not stop passengers walking by and checking for empty beds to use even if you paid for them. Therefore, do not open the cabin door during boarding at stations to avoid this; they also have shutters on cabin doors so no one can enter, and there are locks inside.
