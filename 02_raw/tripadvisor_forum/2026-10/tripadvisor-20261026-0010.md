Mostly meh! Some people do Hanoi to Da Nang at night. Are you aware of how long that ride is? Check the numerous YouTube videos for a visual perspective.

[c:38fd5b29fa]
Whether it is scenic depends on whether you like train travel and whether you like to see the way people really live. Trains go around the back of people's houses and through parts of town that are not tarted up, making very interesting viewing, showing how people really live in their back yards rather than how they showcase houses and businesses from the road at the front. He travelled the whole Hanoi to Saigon run back in 2001 and certainly enjoyed it. He will not say there is that much stunning scenery, but there is certainly plenty to see if you keep your eyes open and that sort of thing interests you. Probably the most scenic part is Hue to Da Nang because you go round the Hai Van Pass. Basically it depends whether you like travelling or just like being somewhere.
