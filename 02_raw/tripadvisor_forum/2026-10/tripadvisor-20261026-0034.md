Train-relevant original-post points: travel south to north in December, starting with warmer weather in the south (Ho Chih Minh / Can Tho), beaches in the middle (Hue / Da Nang), then cool/cold weather in the north in the mountains of Sa Pa. The poster decided to take the train, in particular sleeper trains between major centres, i.e. Ho Chih Minh to Da Nang, Da Nang to Hanoi / Sa Pa. They will probably have to rent a large MPV or get a van with driver to take them around at each centre. Intention is to fly in to Ho Chih Minh and fly out from Hanoi. Two families with young kids: the poster's family with 2 kids, aged 5 and 7, and the brother-in-law's family with 1 kid, aged 6. Budget hotels at US$100 per night per room. Planned train time: probably 1 night taking the train from Ho Chi Minh to Da Nang, another night taking the train from Da Nang to Hanoi, and a day ride from Hanoi to Sa Pa. Arithmetic given: 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 13 nights; 16 days available in total, minus 2 days for flying in and out, leaving 14 days; the extra day should be spent getting back to Hanoi from Sa Pa to catch the flight.
