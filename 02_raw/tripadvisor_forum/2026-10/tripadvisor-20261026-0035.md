Loves train journeys and is travelling to Vietnam for 2 weeks in early June, first time. First train trip will be from Saigon to Da Nang. Has seen lots of YouTube videos, but reviews are mixed about which trains to take. Available options are private Violette or Charmy or the Vietnamese rail coaches. 2-bed VIP accommodation is only available for the 10:15 am departure reaching Da Nang at 5:40 am, but with that option is afraid of missing the scenic route towards Da Nang. On the other hand, a 4-bed option is available for Violette or Charmy or the Vietnamese rail coaches. Asked whether bathrooms are cleaner and better in Violette or Charmy, although mixed reviews have been read on the internet and not much information is available on YouTube. Also confused about train numbers: are SE1, 2, 3 and 4 better than the rest? SE22 appears to have upgraded coaches. Hence very confused about which option to take.
