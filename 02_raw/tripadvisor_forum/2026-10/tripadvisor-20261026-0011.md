Train-relevant parts only; non-train parts of this thread (accommodation in Tam Coc, general itinerary advice) are omitted.

Train-relevant original-post context: returning to Vietnam in June with husband, 3 adult kids, and mum. Itinerary: Hanoi 4 nights, mum and kids departing for Halong Bay overnight, all meeting in Ninh Binh 2 nights, Hanoi 1 night, then originally flying to Da Nang for Hoi An 5 nights, then flying Da Nang to Auckland. The train discussion replaces the Hanoi-to-Da Nang flight leg.

[c:e989ff36b6]
Asks whether they are going back to Hanoi for one night just so they can fly to Da Nang, and whether they have considered getting the train from Ninh Binh to Da Nang.

[c:24873012b8]
Yes, she has been considering it, but is a bit nervous. The group has varying degrees of intrepid and a few would be a bit stressed and get no sleep. She could be convinced.

[c:ea94f044ed]
Night train from Ninh Binh to Danang 100%. Perfectly comfortable and a cool experience. Will save a bunch of time compared to backtracking to Hanoi and navigating airports.

[c:59c8ec4ff5]
If worried about the train journey, maybe the overnight Lotus train would work. The poster did it from Ninh Binh to Da Nang and found it well run and easy, with included snacks and breakfast. It was a treat to see the scenery going past at sunrise. They did not sleep much but it was so exciting that they did not mind.

[c:38fd5b29fa]
Treat the sleeper train as part of the holiday experience. You won't get it in NZ. There is a thrill of boarding a night train, certainly compared to shuffling on board an old B737. Having cycled all the route previously, he took the train from Wellington to Auckland, described as a great trip but missed by so many who want speed and convenience over experience. Don't miss out in Vietnam.
