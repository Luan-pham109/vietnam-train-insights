Or is it better to take one of those mini vans ( we will be 6 traveling together ) during the day? Are the roads safe? Thanks in advance

[c:5c54bef73f]
If the train suits, why not, something different. It does not go to Sapa town itself but to Lao Cai. By road it is mainly dual carriageway from Hanoi to Lao Cai, then sometimes a very twisty road to Sapa town. In February a limo van took just over 5 hours with a 20-minute stop, was OK, no drama, comfy seats, phone charging port, 3 people on a 14-seater.

[c:87ea930f7c]
Train travel in Vietnam is generally better than internet hysteria, comfortable but no true bells and whistles. Meal cart service to most private carriages, plus a general-access dining car. VIP limo vans are preferred for long road distance over sleeper buses, but long road distances in Vietnam are not comfortable, and long stretches after dark are advised against. Daylight travel writes the day off as a travel day. Personally he would take the train, and will do so next week. Bring snacks, with ample opportunity before boarding or a meal nearby.

[c:a87cf9b453]
Take a look at some YouTube videos for a visual perspective.

[c:bc3c6f6fd2]
If you want view and scenery, travel by limo bus. If you want to maximise time, take a train ride; sleeping overnight on the train might be noisy so you will not have a proper sleep, and snacks and food are offered onboard. He recommends dinner before boarding. There are two evening trains, one departing 9pm and one at 10pm.

[c:62688afd5e]
Hi, if you are a group of 6, a limousine van seems the better option offering more flexibility & convenience throughout the journey. The night train trip also includes the transfer from your hotel to/from Hanoi transtation as well as from Lao Cai station to Sapa center, which may be less convenient.

[c:bc3c6f6fd2]
If you can afford a private car, you can see tea farm, cinnamon farm and border gate on the way Hanoi to Lao Cai. On the way back consider the train, because the road Lao Cai to Hanoi is under maintenance so takes longer than usual, around 30 to 40 minutes.

On the claim that Sapa town is overcrowded and full of construction works: big construction in town finished years ago, now creeping down the valley and easily seen on Google Maps satellite view. Chapa Ecolodge is a good choice. Topas is far from town, cable car, glass bridge, shops, restaurants, etc. Better in summer and rice-growing season with spectacular views.

[c:bc3c6f6fd2]
If you want to have nightlife experience, stay around Sapa town, if you just want to relax and chill in the evening after day's activities then stay at Topas Eco lodge, 40 minutes drive from Sapa

[c:f28b57a2f3]
Train journey is an interesting experience and, of course, always safer than travelling by bus. Orient Express is no longer working. Current tourist carriages providing good service include Chapa, Tonkin, Sapaly and King Express. Only night train from Hanoi to Lao Cai, and on return you can choose day or night train. Day train offers a nice view along the rail line.
