I read somewhere that this is a scenic route, with spectacular views between Hue and Da Nang. So maybe it is not a good idea to take the train overnight if one wants to see something?

It will be daylight by the time the train reaches Hue.

[c:f28b57a2f3]
The places the train will pass during the night is nothing to see till the nice places will appear in the morning when you wake up. The most scenic route is definitely from Hue to Da Nang via Hai Van pass overlooking the sea. If stopping in Hue for a visit, then you can hire a car or continue with train to Da Nang.

[c:37e779d0e4]
Don’t miss Hue City. Great history, The Citadel, The Imperial City, Perfume River boat tour, The DMZ tour can be taken from Hue and you can either train along the coast to DaNang, back of a moto from Hue Easyriders or hire a car to go over the Hai Van Pass. Hue has some of VietNam’s best food.

[c:58286ee40c]
I did this overnight train last month. The views after Hue to Da Nang are indeed very picturesque. Though the window I was looking out of wasn’t the cleanest. I would actually consider the question as Plane vs overnight train. The train isn’t a must and if you have limited days I would suggest the plane.
