[c:4148b59271]
Taking the overnight train from Hanoi to Hue is a great choice for the family and, in the poster's opinion, offers a much more comfortable experience compared to sleeper buses. You can book a private 4-berth cabin, which provides more privacy and makes it easier to enjoy the scenic views along the way. You should choose the SE3 and SE19 trains because they are among the newest and most modern, featuring multiple cabins with four berths. The SE3 train departs Hanoi in the evening around 7:30 pm and arrives in Hue early the next morning, around 8:30 am. The SE19 train usually departs around 8:10 pm, arriving in Hue at about nearly 9:05 am. You can book tickets in advance through Baolau or 12Go Asia, which accept international cards. March to April is the peak season for Hue's pleasant weather, so sleeper tickets tend to sell out quickly. Book as early as possible to secure your cabin. Feel free to bring your own snacks for convenience, but also consider trying some local food sold on the train for an authentic experience. Note: this reply's SE19 arrival time (about nearly 9:05 am) differs slightly from reply 13's "about 9.15am"; both are reported as written.
