Travelling 3-4 weeks through Vietnam, wife and himself; their cases will almost certainly exceed 20kg each, which is OK for internal flights; however, they are looking at booking a 2-berth sleeper cabin on the overnight train from Hanoi to Da Nang, and see there is a 20kg maximum weight on baggage; asks whether anyone has experience of taking heavier baggage - is it a relaxed thing (e.g. as would be in their cabin and not affecting anyone else), or does he just pay an extra cost if it is identified, etc.

[c:b9c79d8997]
His wife and he have travelled extensively by train in Vietnam over many years and, whilst they only ever travel with carry-on only, i.e. under 10kg, he has never seen anyone have the size of their luggage challenged and has seen plenty of large bags on board the trains; whilst he would not worry about the bags exceeding the limits, he would be concerned about carrying all that around, on and off trains and buses etc.; it will soon get very tiresome, and there is a risk of someone losing the bags somewhere along the way.

You are very welcome, heartbeat; they travelled the world virtually full time from 2008 until the pandemic and started off with full backpacks but quickly realised it was a nuisance lugging stuff around, much of which they did not use, switched to carry-on only and have travelled that way ever since; as Drifter says above, you can buy stuff in Vietnam if you forget something; there are a couple of posts in their blog which contain their packing lists at https://accidentalnomads.com/category/travel-tips/; they are a bit old now and frankly he finds they take even less now; he is about to depart for a trip to Central America next week so may well try and update his list.

[c:3ec0db9c8d]
Travelling light is the way to go; he uses a small backpack, about 30 litres, weight about 6kg, for a month; this makes life so easy and much cheaper, with no waiting for baggage after flights; laundry is cheap and so are clothes; most things you can buy there now, a few odd things maybe not, dental floss and a certain type of toothpaste; if he did not have a camera, it would be even less.
