We booked our dream journey from south to north on this train in April. They recommend adding at least 2 nights before, in Ho Chi Minh, and 2 nights after, in Hanoi. So we are interested to find out which are the best areas to stay and what are the ‘must see’ in each of these cities.

[c:a87cf9b453]
Must see depends on your interests; which you have not mentioned. You have plenty of time. Do some research! Take a look at Things To o Do in each place; to see what catches your attention. In Hanoi, many people stay in the Old Quarter.

[c:eb95d0fb9a]
Two nights in both HCMC and Hanoi would be selling both places extremely short. Four, or more, would be better. What other places are you planning to stop off in along the way? Scott

[c:a8c926a0e3]
Agree with post #1, check out ‘Things to do’ in each city on TA page and pick what appeal to you.

[c:bc3c6f6fd2]
Hanoi: the Old Quarter, and the area near the Cathedral in smaller streets like Ngo Huyen, Au Trieu, Tho Xuong is quieter, also Duong Thanh street and Nguyen Van To are quieter. Ho Chi Minh City: District 1 is popular, Ky Con is his favourite with local eateries and quiet at night, with better places close to Ben Thanh market, Nguyen Hue walking street or Bui Vien street. Things to do can easily be found in the forum. He loves the coffee apartment in Ho Chi Minh City.
