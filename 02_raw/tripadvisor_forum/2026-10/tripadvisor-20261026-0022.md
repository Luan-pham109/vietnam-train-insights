Has done it by train twice and by car once, both good, train much cheaper. Try to sit on the side of the train by the sea, but you cannot tell what direction the carriage is facing when you book, so it is just luck. On one trip the carriage was quite empty, so even though the booked seat was on the "wrong" side, just moved to a better seat. For the second trip, extravagantly bought tickets for both sides to be sure of sitting on the best side. When going by car, stopped for a short walk around the old buildings at the top of the pass, which was quite interesting and you can appreciate the view. But to be honest, did not find the view anything that amazing from train or car. If going by train, book in advance. Always uses Baolau.com, which is easy.
