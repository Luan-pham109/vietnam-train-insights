Looking for ideas to do a one way travel day trip from Doc Let to Hoi An. We can take a 2 hour taxi back from Doc Let to CXR, then fly to Da Nang then 1 hour to Hoi An, but ideally we would like to do a day train or bus from closer to Doc Let straight to Hoi An. Any ideas that might be better? We don’t want to do a sleeper bus or train for this one. TIA!

[c:bee512d1fb]
The train does not stop in Hoi an- It stops in Danang. You will need a taxi from Doc let to station and then when you arrive in Danang.

[c:464312809a]
From Doc Let to Hoi An, the distance is over 400 kilometers. You need to take a taxi from Doc Let to Nha Trang train station, then take the train from Nha Trang to Da Nang. From there, you can take a taxi to Hoi An.
