Quoting "is there another decent night train option that's cheaper?": "From where to where?" There are more than 3 private rail companies. The route determines the private rail companies available.

[c:2949e93919]
Lotus carriages are attached to the regular VNR train No. SE19 and SE20 which run between Hanoi and Da Nang. They have operated on Hanoi-Lao Cai trains but not at the moment.

[c:a87cf9b453]
Several private companies sell their own 4-berth soft sleeper compartments on trains SE1/SE2/SE3/SE4/SE19 between Hanoi, Hue & Da Nang, aimed at foreign tourists. Livitrans offer soft sleepers on SE1/SE2, Violette offer soft sleepers on SE1/SE2/SE3/SE4, Lotus Train on SE19/SE20. There are other private rail companies on that route, which are also attached to public trains.

[c:2949e93919]
Almost all other private companies' prices are around the same as with Lotus. Suggests sticking with the SE20 and booking a 4-berth cabin on a Vietnam Railway carriage. The Man in Seat 61 does not mention that this train was refurbished in 2023. It should be about as "decent" as you can get. Checked bookings around 4th to 6th August and there are many 4-berth cabins available now. Was surprised, as the SE19 Hanoi to Da Nang train is heavily booked through August. Booking all 4 berths will cost around 5 million VND - around US$200. Only downside is that departure at 6pm will not give much of a look at the coastal scenery just north of Da Nang.

[c:bee512d1fb]
Cannot comment on Lotus, but on a recent trip booked a standard/local 4-berth soft sleeper on 2 trains. SE3 was very clean, modern and slightly longer beds than SE13. SE13 was old and not as clean. Both had clean bedding, food and drinks to purchase, toilet paper provided in toilets. Food service was much more frequent on SE3, but recommends taking own snacks and a towel or sheet to place on the bed. Air conditioning was good in both. A friend recently went on Lotus and air conditioning stopped frequently throughout the night.
