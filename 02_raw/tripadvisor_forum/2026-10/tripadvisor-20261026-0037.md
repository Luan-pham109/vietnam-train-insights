Quoted the night-stop question; of course it stops at night; there are no non-stop trains between Hanoi + Da Nang - how would they make a profit? And yes, people are boarding and departing along the way; perhaps look at the route map + schedule to get an idea of the stops and how long it takes to get there; quoted the lower vs upper berth question; yes, when purchasing tickets you get to select specific berths for each ticket, from what is available, but there may not be 2 lower berths available in the same cabin; link https://www.seat61.com/Vietnam.htm
