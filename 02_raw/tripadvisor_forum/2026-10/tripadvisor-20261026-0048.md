Planning a 4.5-hour journey between train and plane is quite risky; Vietnamese trains are frequently delayed (30-60 minutes), plus an hour to get to Noi Bai Airport and two hours for baggage check-in, which will leave the family extremely rushed and exhausted; advice: reschedule the trip to Sapa to an earlier time or stay overnight in Hanoi on Tuesday to have a leisurely flight on Wednesday morning.
