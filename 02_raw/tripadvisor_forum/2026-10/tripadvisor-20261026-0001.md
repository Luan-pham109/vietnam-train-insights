[c:b9c79d8997]
He has taken overnight sleepers north-south and vice versa several times, always two people in a 4-berth soft sleeper, usually booked in person on arrival. Seat61.com/Vietnam.htm is his go-to resource. Avoid berths 1-4 and 25-28 at carriage ends near toilets and smokers. Toilets are good at the start but can get grim towards the end, so take own paper and wet wipes. Take food, drinks and snacks; train food is hit and miss. He would not leave belongings unattended to eat, and often buys from tea vendors at stations. He linked a 2015 blog post on the Reunification Express Hanoi-Saigon, doubting much had changed.
