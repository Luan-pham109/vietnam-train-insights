Asks if anyone is familiar with the seat layout; can only find conflicting advice online. Hoping that if choosing a seat on the right, e.g. seat 4, that will be best for views. Sorry, not sure about the right-side/left-side thing. If taking the HD4 in the afternoon it probably will not matter, as it is consistently lightly booked while the morning train is very popular and can be booked out. On the HD4 you will probably be able to switch to the "right" side if need be. The Revolution Express is a couple of restored 1960s steam locomotives with period-styled carriages a private company plans to run between Da Nang and Hue; no start date public yet. Check https://dsvn.vn/#/timkiem/ketqua for the seating plans.
