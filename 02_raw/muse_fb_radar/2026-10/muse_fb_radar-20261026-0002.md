Agreeing with the others - paying for an extra ticket under a duplicate or placeholder name is the standard workaround on the regular Vietnam Railways booking. The other option worth comparing is one of the private cabin operators that lease upgraded carriages onto the same trains - several of them sell cabins at a flat private rate rather than per berth, so a family of 5 isn't paying for a stranger's spot either way. We've got a rundown of Hanoi-Sapa transport options including the train here: https://bekatravel.com/.../the-ultimate-sapa-travel-guide... - happy to help if useful. I'm with Beka Travel.
