Ninh Binh to Hanoi train in 2 1/2 hours
My first post in the group
Train from Ninh Binh to Hanoi in Vietnam
Only about a 2 1/2 hour journey
I booked a seat/ bed in a 4 bed sleeper carriage
Not very comfortable seat so not sure it would be a very comfortable sleep
AC and USB power but power didnt work
You can order food using the qr code
Ticket was expensive i thought, about $30 US
https://vnrailway.com/?gad_source=1...

As the name suggests, it's designed as a bed, so it will be more comfortable as a bed than a seat. For journey this short, I would have book a seat, unless it is all sold out, but then i would go and seat at the buffet car and have a drink there too. U$30 is really expensive, where did you get the ticket from? For this price, you probably can travel from Hanoi to Hue, or even Nha Trang!
