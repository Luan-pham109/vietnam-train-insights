Looking to travel Vietnam March 27 and was just doing a little research for a planned Heritage Train trip between Danang and Hue. I've done the Hai Van Pass on a motorbike so was planning to do the train.
Now something that I found, everyone should be aware of.
One site, (vietnamrail) shocked me with their price of USD 44.00.
So another site (vnrailway) was somewhat better with USD 28.08.
What l eventually I found what I think is the true price, through VNPAY app of VND 395000 That's in USD 15.30
All the prices were for Soft seat premium (restaurant/bar)
So both the first two appear to be legitimate (government) sites. It just goes to show......Be Careful
