[c:4e6791a4b3]
For a May trip, I'd aim to book your four-berth soft sleeper as soon as tickets for your travel date are released, especially if you want all four berths together; popular departures can sell out. Vietnam Railways generally releases tickets around 60 days ahead, though longer routes may appear earlier. Fares aren't always fixed: they can vary by train, berth, and booking period, so booking early gives you the best chance of getting your preferred cabin. If you'd like me to arrange it, DM me your travel date and whether you need the whole cabin.
