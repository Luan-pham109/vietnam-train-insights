For a 12:30 pm train, I'd arrange a morning shuttle or private car in advance; the drive from Sapa to Lào Cai station usually takes about 1–1.5 hours, so leaving around 10:30 am gives you a useful buffer. Morning shuttles do run, but schedules vary, so confirm the pickup time when booking. Grab may appear in Sapa, but cars can be scarce and aren't dependable for a train connection.
