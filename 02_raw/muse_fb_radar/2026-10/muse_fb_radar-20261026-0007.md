Yes, it's a great route—about 15-18 hours covering roughly 791km on the Reunification Express line, so most travelers take an overnight sleeper departing evening and arriving the next morning, saving a hotel night in the process. For comfort, a 4-berth soft sleeper (roughly $35-55) gives more space and privacy than the 6-berth hard sleeper, or you can splurge on a 2-berth VIP cabin if budget allows. Booking about 10-15 days ahead is smart, especially around holidays or weekends, since lower bunks and 4-berth compartments sell out fastest. One tip: the train stops at Dong Hoi, gateway to Phong Nha's famous caves—some travelers break the journey there for a day or two before continuing, worth considering if your schedule is flexible. Manage expectations on comfort though—reviews are mixed (older trains, hit-or-miss cleanliness), so it's more about the experience and cost savings than five-star comfort.
