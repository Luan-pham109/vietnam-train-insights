TAKING THE OVERNIGHT TRAIN FROM HANOI TO SAPA? HERE'S WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW BEFORE BOOKING.
If you enjoy train travel, this is a good way to start your Sapa trip. You leave Hanoi at night and arrive in Lao Cai early the next morning, ready to continue your journey into the mountains.
Which train should you choose?
The regular Vietnam Railways train is a practical option if you want to save money. You can book a sleeper berth, with four-bed soft sleeper cabins generally offering more space and comfort than six-bed compartments.
If you prefer something a little more comfortable, you can also look at private tourist carriages attached to the train, such as Sapaly Express, Chapa Express and Laman Express. These usually have more carefully decorated cabins, but you'll pay more.
What is the journey like?
The train usually leaves Hanoi in the evening and arrives in Lao Cai early the following morning. The journey takes around 8 hours, although schedules vary.
Don't expect a luxury hotel on wheels. The train can be noisy and bumpy, and the facilities vary between carriages. Bring earplugs if you're a light sleeper, plus some water and snacks for the journey.
Don't forget the last part of the trip.
The train stops at Lao Cai, not Sapa town. From Lao Cai Station, you'll need another transfer to Sapa, usually around an hour by road depending on traffic and weather.
You can take a shared bus, taxi or private car. If you arrive early in the morning, arranging your transfer in advance can save you some hassle.
A few things worth remembering: Book ahead during busy travel periods, particularly if you want a specific berth. Arrive at Hanoi Railway Station with enough time to find your departure platform and carriage. Keep your passport, phone and valuables close to you. Check whether meals or drinks are included in your ticket. Ask your Sapa hotel if you can leave your luggage or arrange early check-in when you arrive.
