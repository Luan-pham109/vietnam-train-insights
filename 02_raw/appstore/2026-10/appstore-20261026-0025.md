I bought train tickets in Laos expecting to receive electronic tickets. There was no indication during booking that the tickets would not be delivered electronically Instead, I got a message saying I had to go to location that does not exist on Google maps. The map link provided by the app also did not function. I complained to this app about this deceptive practice. They basically told me I have to go to location 2 km from the train station to get my tickets. This will necessitate an unnecessary trip and incredible inconvenience. This is the last time I will ever use this app for purchases.
