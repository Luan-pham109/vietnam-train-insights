Small heritage train station on the route to Da Nang with I think, per day, 8 regular commuter trains (taking 2.5 to 3 hours, trolly service for food) and two special tourist heritage trains (taking 3.5 to 4 hours, cultural car with food and a show). Both take a highly scenic route to Da Nang.

On departure: Passengers congregate in the (smallish) waiting area until an announcement is made, and then staff will check tickets as people go through the door to the platform. The departures board displays planned departure and arrival times, estimated departure and arrival times, and any delay. There are a street vendors outside with water, packages snacks and banh mi.

On arrival: it is a short walk into the centre of town and the walk along the Perfume River is lovely. If needing transport, aside from booking via Grab, there are lots of taxis and rideshare drivers outside touting for customers who will be willing to negotiate a cash fare (you can use the Grab app to check a fair rate for your destination).
