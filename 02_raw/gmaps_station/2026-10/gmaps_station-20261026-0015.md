Great location to down town. Have your tickets ready and super easy to board. I got there early expecting similar situation as airports. Checked in and boarded within 5 minutes. Not many options to buy snacks and drinks for train ride so bring what you need with you. Staff are not very helpful. you need to push them for assistance.
