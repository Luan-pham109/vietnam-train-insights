Nha Trang Station is a small but functional train station. It's not fancy, but it gets the job done. The staff are helpful, and the location is convenient, right in the city center, close to restaurants and hotels. Trains usually run on time, and the waiting area is clean, though a bit basic. Good enough if you're catching a train, but don’t expect anything special.
