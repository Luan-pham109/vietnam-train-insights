The exterior and interior of the station have a wonderful atmosphere. For those who love vintage vibes, this space is incredibly charming. However, the waiting area is quite cramped, and the persistent soliciting by taxi drivers at the entrance slightly detracts from the experience. That's a bit of a downside.
