Used this service from Hanoi to Ninh Binh in May 2026. There is a coffee and snack stand at the station with a wide variety of drinks available. However, the snacks seemed to be microwave-heated and were not very tasty, so I would recommend getting takeaway food from a nearby restaurant instead. There is also a waiting area on the opposite side where you can relax if you buy something. Beer is available too. The toilets at the station were average but smelled quite bad. On the train, staff constantly came through selling mango, drinks, bánh mì, and other snacks, which was convenient. The toilets on the train were clean at first, but later in the journey they apparently became extremely smelly and even overflowed with water. A friend of mine brought a portable toilet just in case. The train departed and arrived on time.
