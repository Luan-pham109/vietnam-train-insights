Took the train here from Danang to hue with the heritage train. Polite staff and easy onboarding process, however note if you have some big luggage, a male staff may seem very enthusiastic about helping you put on the trolley and on to the train when boarding, however he will demand a tip which was unexpected as he is a train staff.
