Was here twice recently in February, once on arrival from Da Nang around 12.30pm and the other for departure back to Da Nang at 6.35am.
The departure waiting lounge is not large and was not crowded at that time but may not have sufficient seats during peak hour.

The tourist train was comfortable enough and the views of the sea were nice.
Worth taking the ride if have time.

The review for the trip from Da Nang to Hue on a normal stage train is posted on the Da Nang station.
