I forgot my dji drone in the train due to having to run around after my son and helping my wife with bags, when I realised my mistake I went to the trian station to report the loss. The staff where super helpful, they called the train which was still heading south towards Saigon. Within 20 minutes they found it, and arranged to return it by 5pm the same day when the train was making its return trip.

10/10 to the staff for all their support
