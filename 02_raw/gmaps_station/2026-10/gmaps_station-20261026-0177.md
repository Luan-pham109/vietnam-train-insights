This was my second time using Vietnam Railways, traveling from Ho Chi Minh City to Da Nang and from Da Nang to Hanoi. I was impressed with the safety standards, the calm and well organized stations, and the clean restrooms. The staff were helpful and provided clear information about the train tickets. Booking through the website was also easy and convenient. If I have the chance, I would definitely use Vietnam Railways again.
