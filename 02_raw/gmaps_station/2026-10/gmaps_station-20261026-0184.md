The station is small and busy and can get quite crowded, especially during peak hours. The waiting room has no air-conditioning, so it can feel hot and stuffy, particularly in summer. Facilities are basic but functional. To avoid long waits in the heat, it’s better to arrive just 20–30 minutes before your train departs.
