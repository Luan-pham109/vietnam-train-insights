Cool historic station with a unique mix of architectural styles. It's functional, easy to navigate, and right in the city center. Buying tickets is straightforward, and the staff are helpful enough. It gets quite busy and loud before train departures, but it's all part of the authentic Vietnamese travel experience. Definitely book your tickets in advance online if you can!
