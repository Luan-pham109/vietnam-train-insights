Hue Railway Station may not be the biggest or most spectacular station in Vietnam, but it has a lovely old-world charm and is a great place to experience a more authentic side of the country.

The station has a fascinating history and retains some of its French colonial character. Watching the trains arrive and seeing the everyday hustle and bustle of local travellers is part of the experience.

For us, it was more than just a place to catch a train — it was part of the adventure and a great way to see a different side of Vietnam. 🚂🇻🇳
