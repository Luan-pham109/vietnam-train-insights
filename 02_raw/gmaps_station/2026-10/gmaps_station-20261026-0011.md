We took the overnight train from Hanoi to Sapa. It was an experience but I wouldn't recommend it, too noisy and you won't get a good sleep. The VIP lounge at the station was complimentary but the ticket said we could go there at 8 pm but we were told to come back at 9 unless we wanted to pay. We used the luggage lockers (outside the VIP area) which were great, really spacious and convenient. On our return from Sapa, at 5.30 a.m., a lady from the VIP lounge directed us away from the lockers saying they were full. She directed us towards the VIP Lounge and wanted us again to pay for bag storage there. Luckily my husband went to check the lockers and they were not full so we stored our luggage there again and would recommend avoiding the 'VIP lounge' entirely at the Hanoi Railway Station.
