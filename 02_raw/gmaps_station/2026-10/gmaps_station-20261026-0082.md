This is a small and quiet railway station in Lao Cai with very few trains, mostly just two going to Hanoi daily and a few to nearby towns. Since the local population is low, it doesn't get crowded. Buses are more frequent, cheaper, and available anytime from the bus stand right in front of the station. Trains are costlier and must be booked in advance. I didn't get the ticket to experience the train journey, so I travelled by bus to Hanoi. The area around the station is calm and peaceful, with hardly anyone around during the day.
