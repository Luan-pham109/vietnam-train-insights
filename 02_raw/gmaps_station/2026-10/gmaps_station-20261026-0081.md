Wow, I can't believe how this place completely ruined what was supposed to be a special trip to Vietnam with my friends. I've never been scammed so obviously before. We tried to buy train tickets at the station, but they told us—actually, forced us—to pay for FOUR people, even though there were only THREE of us. And to make it worse, the price kept changing every few minutes for no reason! It didn't stop there. They actually dragged us to an ATM and pressured us to pay these ridiculous, constantly changing prices. After the train had already left, they even suggested we should still pay and then take a taxi to the next station to "catch up" with the train. Seriously? When we decided to ignore them and tried to get our own taxi, they chased after us, took the taxi driver's phone number, and kept calling him. I have no idea what they were planning, but it felt really threatening and made us feel very unsafe. If anyone is thinking about using this place—BE CAREFUL. I really hope no one else has to go through something like this. And to the people running this scam: please try having some basic decency next time.
