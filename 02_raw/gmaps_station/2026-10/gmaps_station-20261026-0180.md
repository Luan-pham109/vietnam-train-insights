The staff were very helpful as our train was cancelled and assisted with a new train tickets (cash only). Also helped with storing our luggage for the day.
Tiny train station with an Agribank Atm (not listed) about 300m away. Walk out the station, past the steam engine, cross the road, walk 1 block, turn right and the bank is across the road. A great cafe and bakery across the road.
