Small train station that can get crowded. You may need to drag your luggage across train tracks, so if you have large or heavy bags/luggage, you may be in for a challenge.

When arriving, there will be many cab drivers offering to give you a ride. I recommend only using Grab. It is the best way to travel in Hue and most of Vietnam.
