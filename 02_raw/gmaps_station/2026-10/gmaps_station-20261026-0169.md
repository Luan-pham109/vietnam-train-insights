Functional but Nothing Special – Watch Out for the Matcha Prices!
If you are traveling through this station, don't expect too much. It’s a small, basic station that gets the job done, but it’s not particularly comfortable.
Facilities: The station is quite small and unfortunately, the toilets were not clean. It’s definitely not a place where you want to spend more time than necessary.
Boarding Process: Be aware that you cannot go onto the platforms early. The doors to the tracks only open once the train has actually arrived and is standing at the platform.
Food & Drink: There is a cafe stand inside, but I wouldn't recommend it. I paid 70,000 VND for a Matcha, which was far too expensive for what it was—especially compared to prices elsewhere in Vietnam.
It’s a typical small train station. Show up, get your ticket, but maybe skip the expensive drinks and wait until you're at your destination for a clean restroom!
